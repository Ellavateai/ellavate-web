Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

This page describes how Ellavate AI AB processes personal data about you when you visit ellavate.ai or contact us. How we process data on behalf of our customers in the services is governed by the data processing agreement with each customer.

Who is responsible

Ellavate AI AB, reg. no. 559608-2676, registered office in Stockholm, Sweden, is the data controller. Contact: info@ellavate.ai.

What we process

  • Name, company and email address that you send yourself when you book a demo or email us.
  • What you write in your contact with us.
  • Technical information that always accompanies a visit to a website, such as IP address, in the hosting provider's logs.

Why, and on what basis

We use the data to reply to you, book and hold the demo and follow up. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries from companies (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not sell the data or use it for advertising.

How long

We keep the data for as long as the contact is ongoing and delete it no later than 24 months after the last contact. If you become a customer, the agreement and Swedish accounting law apply.

Who helps us

  • Microsoft, which provides our email.
  • GitHub, which serves the website and therefore receives your IP address when the page loads. GitHub is a US company; transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and tracking

The website uses no cookies, no visitor analytics and no tracking tools. The fonts are served from our own address.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to the processing or ask for it to be restricted. Email info@ellavate.ai and we will reply within one month. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, imy.se.
