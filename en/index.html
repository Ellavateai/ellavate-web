Common questions.

Where is the data stored?

The service and the database run in the EU, in Frankfurt. The language model comes from Anthropic, which processes the question in the US under the EU standard contractual clauses and does not use it to train its models. It is all set out in the data processing agreement you receive before go-live.

What happens when the regulation changes?

We monitor the Swedish Transport Agency and load the change. The answer then shows what changed and from which date, so nobody has to read a consultation paper to keep up.

Can the answer be wrong?

The handbook quotes the regulation word for word and shows paragraph and page, so the technician checks the source in one tap. It does not make up rules of its own. The assessment is always the technician's.

How quickly are we up and running?

The regulation is already loaded. Your branding, your address and your links are in place within a day. Your own material is loaded after that.

Do we need to install anything?

No. The handbook opens in the browser on handhelds, tablets, phones and computers.

Can we add our own material?

Yes. Internal instructions and make-and-model material are loaded and shown next to the regulation. They stay yours and are never shared with other customers.

What does it cost?

One monthly fee per company, with no setup fee. The price depends on the number of stations and the modules you choose. Book a demo and you get a quote.
